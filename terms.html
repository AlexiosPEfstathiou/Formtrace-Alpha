2. Words we use

  • Trainee – a person who posts a goal and receives coaching.
  • Coach – a person who offers coaching services through FormTrace.
  • Goal – a trainee's request for coaching, with its duration in weeks.
  • Offer – a coach's proposal for a goal, including a weekly price.
  • Engagement – the coaching arrangement that exists once a trainee accepts an offer.
  • Week – one week of an engagement. A week is delivered when it has closed and the coach has reviewed the sessions the trainee submitted in it [confirm the exact rule used by the weekly release job].
  • Deposit – the total price of an engagement, paid by the trainee to FormTrace when accepting an offer.
  • Release – a payment from the deposit to the coach for a delivered week.

3. Who can use FormTrace

  • You must be 18 or over. FormTrace records progress photos and videos, so we do not accept younger users. You confirm your date of birth when you first sign in; giving a false date is a breach of these terms and we will close the account.

  • You must be able to enter into a binding contract and, as a coach, be legally entitled to provide the services you offer in your country.

4. What FormTrace is, and is not

FormTrace is a platform that connects trainees with coaches, hosts the recordings and reviews they exchange, and handles payment between them. Coaches are independent; they are not employed by FormTrace and FormTrace does not supervise their advice.

For payments, FormTrace acts as the merchant of record: the trainee pays FormTrace, FormTrace holds the deposit and pays the coach as weeks are delivered. The coaching contract itself is between the trainee and the coach, on the terms of the accepted offer and this document.

FormTrace does not provide medical, nutritional or physiotherapy advice (see section 11).

7. Payments, deposits and releases

Payments are processed by Stripe. FormTrace never sees your card number. Stripe's own terms apply to your use of its checkout.

  1. Deposit. When a trainee accepts an offer they pay the whole engagement price to FormTrace. FormTrace holds it on the trainee's behalf; it is not FormTrace's revenue.
  2. Weekly release. After each week is delivered, that week's share of the deposit (weekly price less the coach's commission) is paid to the coach. The payment tracker in the app shows every release with its date.
  3. Payment processing line. Checkout may show a separate "payment processing" amount that passes on the card and payout costs charged by Stripe, rounded up to the whole euro. It is shown before you pay and is not refundable for weeks already delivered. [Keep only if EA ships.]
  4. Platform fees. Any trainee service fee or coach commission is shown in the app before you commit. During the pilot both are 0%.
  5. Currency. All prices are in euro (EUR). Your bank may charge conversion fees.
  6. Receipts. Stripe emails a receipt for each payment; the tracker is the record of releases and refunds.
