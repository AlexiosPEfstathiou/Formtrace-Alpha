What FormTrace stores about you
To coach you, this app records: videos of you exercising, with sound, weekly progress photos, body measurements and weight, and what you eat.
Your coach can see this while you work together. Nobody else can — it isn't public, isn't sold, and isn't used for advertising. Two exceptions, both deliberate: videos you post to the marketplace are visible to coaches browsing for trainees, and your age is shown on your profile to anyone who views it.
Your date of birth itself is never shown or shared — only the age calculated from it.
You can delete your account at any time from Profile. That permanently removes your photos, videos, food logs and measurements, and strips your name from anything shared.
This is an early-access build. Don't record anything you wouldn't want a coach to see.
You must be 18 or over to use FormTrace, because it records progress photos.
